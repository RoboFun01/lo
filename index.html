Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026

What this site is

A Grade 11 Life Orientation school assignment. It is a single static HTML file. No server, no database, no accounts, no sign-in.

What is collected

Nothing. This page sets no cookies, runs no analytics, has no forms and tracks nobody. It stores nothing in your browser. Three things do happen outside this page:

  • Fonts are loaded from Fontshare and Google Fonts. Those services see your IP address at the moment they serve the font.
  • One image, the aerial photograph of Tygerberg Hospital, is loaded from Wikimedia Commons. That service sees your IP address when it serves the image.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the file and keeps standard server logs, which are not accessible to me.

Nothing you do on this page is sent anywhere. Opening a photograph, scrolling, or following a map link is not recorded.

Search engines

This site asks search engines not to index it. It is coursework, not a publication.

Locations

Each interview shows the town or city where it took place and links to a map. No street address of a private workplace is given.

Photographs

Most photographs were taken by me during the three visits. Every other image carries a visible badge and was not taken by me.

  • Images badged "Dragonfly-foto" come from Dragonfly Aerospace's own website and remain their property. They are used here to show the facility seen during the visit, and will be removed on request.
  • The badged medical images come from Unsplash and are freely licensed.
  • The aerial photograph of Tygerberg Hospital is by Dfmalan, used under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence, and cropped for this page.

POPIA

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (Act 4 of 2013), this page processes no personal information about visitors.

Removal

If you want anything on this page removed, email me and it will be removed.
